# NNNN. Title in one line

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true that forces a decision. Constraints, the problem, what we know and what we do not. No solution here.

## Decision

What we decided, in the present tense and in one or two sentences. Then the details that a reader needs in order to
follow it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to live with. Include the cost we accepted knowingly.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative in a line or two, and the reason it lost. If an alternative is still viable, say what would make us
switch to it.
