# NNNN. What we are trying

- Status: open | closed
- Started: YYYY-MM-DD
- Closed: YYYY-MM-DD

## Hypothesis

One falsifiable sentence. What we believe, stated so that it can turn out to be wrong.

## Setup

What we are building or changing in order to find out, and how little of it we can get away with.

## What we will look at

The observations that would confirm or refute the hypothesis. For a feel question, say what we will watch a player do.
For a load question, say the number and the tool.

## Observations

What actually happened. Written as it happens, not reconstructed afterwards.

## Result

Confirmed, refuted, or inconclusive, and what surprised us.

## Decision

Keep, kill or pivot, and the reason. If this changes the vision, the architecture, an event or a term, name the
document that changes with it.

## Follow-ups

What this opened up. New experiments, new decision records, or work for the roadmap.
