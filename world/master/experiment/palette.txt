The experimental terrain palette: 32 classes
============================================

One flat colour per terrain class in terrain.png, which is an 8-bit indexed PNG of 16384 by 8192,
unprojected: longitude runs -180 to 180 left to right, latitude 90 to -90 top to bottom. One pixel is
about 2.4 km north to south, and 2.4 km east to west at the equator.

The colours are DATA, not a look. They exist so a person can tell classes apart while painting and so
the importer can snap any pixel back to exactly one class. What a forest looks like in the game is the
renderer's business. No two classes are closer than 40 apart in straight-line RGB distance
(closest pair: taiga-open and swamp-forest).

Paint with the Pencil, not the Brush. Anti-aliasing off, bucket tolerance zero, interpolation set to
Nearest Neighbour. See docs/AUTHORING-THE-WORLD.md.

id  name                  hex       kind   what it means
--  --------------------  --------  -----  -------------------------------------------------------------
 0  ocean                 #104058   water  Open sea. The base colour of the image: anywhere no land layer painted.
 1  shallow-sea           #207098   water  Continental shelf, inside the 200 m isobath. Sea a ship can anchor in.
 2  lake                  #2890d0   water  Standing fresh water. Every lake down to 10 ha gets at least one pixel.
 3  river                 #80d0f0   water  Running water. Every reach of Strahler order three and up, drawn one to three pixels wide by size.

 4  ice                   #f0f8f8   land   Permanent ice: ice caps, ice sheets, glaciers, shelf ice.
 5  polar-desert          #c8c8d8   land   Bare frozen ground with almost nothing growing on it. High arctic gravel and rock.
 6  tundra                #b0a8c8   land   Moss, lichen and dwarf shrub over permafrost. Treeless but alive.

 7  taiga                 #105028   land   Closed boreal conifer forest. Dark, dense, unbroken.
 8  taiga-open            #407848   land   Thin boreal forest: lichen woodland, bog margins, trees with sky between them.
 9  conifer-forest        #187850   land   Temperate needleleaf forest. Pine and spruce south of the taiga.
10  broadleaf-forest      #68c048   land   Temperate deciduous forest. Oak, beech, birch.
11  mixed-forest          #48a038   land   Temperate forest of both kinds together, which is most of northern Europe.
12  rainforest            #008070   land   Closed tropical moist broadleaf forest.
13  dry-forest            #90b038   land   Tropical and subtropical dry forest, and open woodland with a grass floor.
14  mangrove              #306068   land   Salt-tolerant coastal forest standing in tidal water.
15  swamp-forest          #405830   land   Forest standing in fresh water: flooded forest, carr, varzea.

16  steppe                #d0d848   land   Dry temperate grassland. Prairie, pampas, the Eurasian steppe.
17  meadow                #a8d878   land   Moist grassland and pasture. Grass that is grazed rather than ploughed.
18  savanna               #e08830   land   Tropical grassland with scattered trees.
19  scrub                 #a89850   land   Shrubland: maquis, garrigue, chaparral, xeric scrub. Waist-high and woody.
20  heath                 #887088   land   Cool oceanic moor and heath. Heather, gorse, wind, no trees.

21  farmland              #f0e060   land   Ploughed and managed land, open field to the horizon.
22  farmland-with-woods   #b0c058   land   The mosaic: fields with woodlots, hedges and copses between them. Most of settled Europe is this.
23  paddy                 #80d0a0   land   Flooded cropland. Rice terraces and delta paddies.

24  marsh                 #607028   land   Treeless freshwater wetland: reed bed, fen, sedge marsh.
25  bog                   #685040   land   Peatland and mire. Acid, spongy, cold. The Swedish and Finnish mosse.
26  floodplain            #a8c8a0   land   Seasonally flooded grassland. Dry half the year, under water the other half.

27  salt-flat             #e8e8c0   land   Playa, salar, sabkha. Flat, white, and lethal to cross.
28  desert-sand           #f0d898   land   Sand desert. Dunes and erg, nothing growing at all.
29  desert-stone          #c8a878   land   Stony desert and sparse xeric ground: reg, hamada, gravel plain.

30  alpine-meadow         #c0e0b8   land   Montane grassland above the treeline but below the rock.
31  bare-rock             #886850   land   Scree, cliff and bare mountain stone.
